TOURISTINBD - WHAT CHANGED ON 27 OCTOBER 2026
===============================================

Written for the team and the supervisor. Plain language, no code. The work below
was done by Unaiza on top of the 15,567 Google Maps reviews Yakin collected.

In one paragraph
----------------
The hidden-gem engine now reads Bangla, understands what is a place and what is
a food or an activity, and no longer calls famous places "hidden". Before this
work it found no foods, no activities and nothing written in Bangla script, and
its number-one "gem" was the tip "Best Time to Visit". Now it finds 918 food
mentions, 2,037 activity mentions and 2,178 mentions written in Bangla, every one
traceable to the exact sentence in a real review. Each place has a card of what
it offers, a dozen local food specialities were found (Kushtia kulfi, Bogura
kotkoti, Sreemangal seven-layer tea...), and every gem now has a point on a map
and a label saying how recent its evidence is. No review was changed.

WHAT WAS DONE
=============

Part 1 - Places, foods and activities, in Bangla too
----------------------------------------------------

1. Only places are ranked as gems
   - Before, tips like "Best Time to Visit" and "visit early in the morning" were
     scored as if they were places, and the tip came first.
   - Now foods, activities, transport and tips are not gems. They describe the
     place the review is about, and appear on that place's card.

2. Foods and activities are found, from real review text
   - A reviewed word list (data/food_activity_lexicon.csv): about 45 foods and 20
     activities, each in English, Banglish and Bangla spellings ("fuchka",
     "ফুচকা"; "boat ride", "নৌকা ভ্রমণ").
   - Every spelling was checked by counting it in the reviews and reading the
     sentences before it was accepted.
   - Words that mislead are kept in the list as REJECTED, with the reason written
     down, so the decision can be checked:
       পান   means "get / drink" far more often than betel leaf
       আম    (mango) would match inside আমি ("I")
       ভেলা   (raft) is mostly the phrase মেঘের ভেলা, "a raft of clouds"
       crab   is mostly the red crabs on beaches, not food

3. Bangla names of places are recognised
   - 171 Bangla spellings were mined from the Bangla reviews themselves by matching
     their sound to known place names. 134 were accepted, 37 rejected with reasons
     (লোকাল "local" sounds like Lalakhal; পসরা "wares"; বর্ষার "of the monsoon").
     File: data/place_names_bn.csv.
   - Bangla word endings are handled: বান্দরবানের, আলুটিলায়, ইলিশের are found as
     বান্দরবান, আলুটিলা, ইলিশ. A word inside another word is never matched.
   - A Bangla review mentioning its own place (বান্দরবান in a Bandarban review) is
     not counted as a discovery, the same rule as for English.
   - Effect: Gangamati went from 6 to 12 supporting reviews, because half of its
     evidence was in Bangla and was invisible before.

4. Place cards and local specialities
   - For each of 104 places: the foods, activities, transport and tips its reviews
     mention, with counts and one real sentence for each
     (data/phase4_place_attributes.csv).
   - The 12 local specialities above (data/phase4_local_specialities.csv).

Part 2 - Famous places no longer called hidden; gems on the database and map
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. A serious error found and fixed: famous places were ranked as "undocumented"
   - Several "V0 - on no map" gems were places we had scraped from Google Maps
     ourselves: T-Badh (7,902 Google reviews), Bichnakandi (5,263, spelled
     "Bisnakandi"), Kantajew Temple (4,827, spelled "Kantaji Temple"), Mohamaya
     Lake, Karamjal, Bijoypur Lake, I-Badh. OpenStreetMap simply spells them
     differently.
   - The fix uses what the v2 plan already asks for in section 5.4: a candidate's
     Google Maps presence and review count. A place on our own place list is on a
     map, so it is never V0. Its level comes from its Google review count:
     1,000+ is V3, 100-999 is V2, fewer is V1.
   - This rule can only make a place LESS hidden, never more, so it removes false
     discoveries and cannot create new ones.
   - Spelling differences (Kantaji / Kantajew) are proposed automatically but only
     used once a person accepts them (data/place_spellings.csv: 5 accepted, each
     with a reason; one review even says "Kantaji Temple or Kantajew Temple").
   - 29 places moved up a level. 12 V0 places became 3.

6. How recent the evidence is
   - The reviews go back to 2011, so a place card could quote a 2017 bus fare.
   - Every gem now shows the year of its newest evidence and is marked "recent"
     (a review in the last 3 years) or "old". Old gems stay listed, because the
     evidence is real, but are flagged "needs recent confirmation". Today: 3 of 54
     (Hiron Point, Puthia, I-Badh).
   - Place cards quote a recent review whenever one exists, and transport or tips
     with no recent review are marked out of date. 326 of the 1,064 card rows
     have no review from the last 3 years.
   - The 3-year window is the same one the gem score already used, kept in one
     place so the two can never disagree.
   - Checked on the data: 89% of reviews are from 2018 or later and only 11 are
     older than 2016. The Bangla share has been steady at about 25% every year
     since 2019, so it does not drift with time.

7. Gems, place cards and specialities are in the database and the API
   - Database version 3: three new tables (gems, place_attributes,
     local_specialities), map coordinates for the 100 listed places, and two
     views that work out freshness automatically.
   - New API endpoints (all read-only):
       /api/gems                 ranked gems; filter by level, division, freshness
       /api/gems/map             map data: gem points and the 100 places
       /api/gems/{id}            one gem with its sentences, newest first
       /api/place-attributes     what a place offers, with real sentences
       /api/local-specialities   the specialities
   - Every map point says where it came from: OpenStreetMap (36), our Google Maps
     place list (15), or - for the 3 places on no map at all - "near" the place
     whose reviews mention them (Gangamati and Fatrar Chor near Kuakata).
   - Madhabpur Lake has no point: placing it "near" the review that mentions it
     would have put it in the wrong district, so it is left off and listed as
     unmapped instead.

8. Other mistakes found and fixed along the way
   - The name merger crashed on a Bangla/English pair; fixed.
   - Bangla word endings with য় failed to match because of two ways of writing
     the same letter; fixed.
   - Seven duplicate entities were merged (for example "Padma" had two IDs). Their
     old IDs are kept as pointers, so nothing that refers to them breaks.
